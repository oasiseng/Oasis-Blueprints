Panel notes

  1. Wire sizes are copper, 60 C NM-B ampacity unless noted; equipment grounding conductors per NEC Table 250.122.
  2. AFCI / GFCI is printed where it is provided: inside the breaker (dual-function or GFCI breaker), beside the outlet (GFCI receptacle), or as "required" beside the circuit while the method is still open.
  3. Disconnects within sight of the water heater and the mini-split outdoor unit.
  4. Surge protective device, Type 1 or Type 2, at the service equipment (NEC 230.67).
  5. Provide a typewritten circuit directory (NEC 408.4(A)).
